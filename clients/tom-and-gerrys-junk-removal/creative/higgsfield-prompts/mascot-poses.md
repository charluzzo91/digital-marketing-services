## Per-pose requirements
- Identical wardrobe, palette and proportions across every pose — check against the sheet.
- Plain flat background, no scene.
- Generous margin; nothing cropped.
- Full body unless the pose is explicitly a head/bust.
- Same outline weight and flat-colour treatment throughout.

## Rejection triggers
Proportions drifted · head grew · eyes got larger or glossier · outline weight changed ·
extra colours appeared · gradients or shading crept in · reads as any existing franchise character.
