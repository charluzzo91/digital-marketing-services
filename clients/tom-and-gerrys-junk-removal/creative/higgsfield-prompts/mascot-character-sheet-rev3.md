## THE PROBLEM

v2's face reads **sharp, severe and slightly menacing**. The brief calls for *masculine without
feeling aggressive*, *approachable*, *a slight smirk*. He currently scowls.

Diagnosis, in order of impact:

| # | Cause | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Inner ends of the brows angle DOWN toward the nose** | Universal anger signal. Doing most of the damage. |
| 2 | **Narrow eyes, pupils pushed to the side** | Looking past the viewer — suspicious, smug |
| 3 | **Long pointed snout** | Reads **RAT**, not mouse. Bad adjacency for a junk company: vermin, garbage |
| 4 | Angular jaw, hard cheek cut | Severity |
| 5 | Long stiff straight whiskers, thin mouth line | Becomes a sneer at small sizes |

## THE FIX — face and snout only

**1. BROWS — the most important change.** Flip the angle. The inner ends must sit **level with or
slightly HIGHER than the outer ends**. Thick and characterful is fine — keep them bold — but the
inner tips must NOT dip toward the nose. Relaxed and confident, not furrowed.

**2. EYES.** Open them up — noticeably rounder and larger than v2. Pupils **centred, looking
straight at the viewer**, not sidelong. Warm and direct.

**3. SNOUT — shorten it.** Roughly **two-thirds of v2's length**, with a **rounded blunt tip**,
not a point. He should read **MOUSE, not rat.** Keep the nose small and rounded.

**4. JAW AND CHEEK.** Round off the angular cut. Softer, fuller cheek curve. The head silhouette
should be built from rounded forms, no sharp corners anywhere.

**5. WHISKERS.** Fewer — three per side. Shorter, with a **gentle downward curve**, not long stiff
straight spikes.

**6. MOUTH.** A slightly wider, clearly **upward-curving** closed half-smile, lifting at both
corners. Friendly and self-assured. Not a thin straight smirk, not a grin, no visible teeth.

**Target read:** a capable, good-natured workman who is pleased to help — the guy you're relieved
to see pull up. NOT tough, NOT suspicious, NOT scowling.
